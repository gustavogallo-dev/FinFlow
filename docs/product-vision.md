# FinFlow

## Product Vision

## Overview

### What is FinFlow?

FinFlow is a modern Software as a Service (SaaS) platform designed to simplify financial management for small and medium-sized businesses.

### What does it do?

The platform centralizes financial operations into a single, intuitive environment, allowing business owners to track cash flow, manage financial transactions, and make data-driven decisions through real-time insights.

### What makes FinFlow different?

Rather than replacing complex ERP solutions, FinFlow focuses on delivering an accessible, user-friendly experience that helps businesses gain complete visibility over their financial health.
